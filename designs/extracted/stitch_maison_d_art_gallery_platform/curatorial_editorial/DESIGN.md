## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the hushed reverence, architectural spatiality, and discerning discretion of an elite private gallery. Built for high-net-worth collectors, institutional curators, and connoisseurs of original contemporary and classical fine art, the aesthetic deliberately distances itself from transactional e-commerce mechanics. 

The visual strategy blends **Architectural Minimalism** with **Haute Editorial Publishing**:
- **Purity of Presentation:** The interface acts as museum white-space (rendered in warm organic ivory rather than sterile digital white), yielding complete visual dominance to the artworks.
- **Restraint over Frictionlessness:** Fast-fashion UI conventions (such as pulsing discount tags, countdown timers, and neon CTA buttons) are entirely eliminated in favor of serene pacing, contemplative browsing, and quiet authority.
- **Architectural Rhythm:** Asymmetrical compositions, intentional whitespace as an active compositional tool, and micro-fine hairline dividers evoke the spatial elegance of gallery floor plans and premium archival catalogs.

## Colors

The chromatic palette is grounded in mineral and tactile materials—warm limestone, unbleached cotton rag, and patinated bronze.

- **Primary Canvas & Surfaces:** 
  - Base Ground: `#FAF8F5` (Warm Ivory / Alabaster) provides a tactile, glare-free gallery wall.
  - Surface Substrates: `#F2EFE9` (Soft Stone / Travertine) defines secondary containers, framing bays, and modal elevations without artificial brightness steps.
  - Borders & Fillets: `#E6E2DA` delivers imperceptible architectural structure through razor-thin borders.
- **Typography & Structural Inks:**
  - Deep Ink: `#111110` (Off-black) reserved for primary titles, display headings, and core statements.
  - Deep Charcoal: `#1A1918` utilized for body typography, artwork metadata titles, and primary control labels.
  - Patina Neutral: `#78746F` dedicated to secondary metadata, medium labels, physical dimensions, and publication citations.
- **Precious Accents:**
  - Brushed Bronze: `#A38A5E` and Antique Gold: `#8C734B` are employed with extreme restraint—reserved for provenance seals, authentic certifier signatures, active acquisition states, and curated curation flags. They should never be used as solid high-contrast backgrounds for standard buttons.

## Typography

Typography acts as the primary curator of the brand experience, counterbalancing literary classicism with mid-century modernist Swiss discipline.

- **Headline & Display Tier (`Playfair Display`):** Evokes the provenance of rare monographs and auction house retrospectives. Set with graceful, open line heights and relaxed or optical tight tracking depending on scale. Never set in heavy bold weights; structural emphasis is achieved through scale, italicization, and placement.
- **Editorial & Body Tier (`Manrope`):** Delivers neutral, pristine legibility for curatorial essays, artist statements, and provenance chronologies. Light and regular weights preserve whitespace and prevent visual heaviness.
- **Cataloguing & Label Tier (`Manrope` uppercase):** Monographic classifications (e.g., medium, dimensions, year of creation, certificate status) are set in uppercase, tracking between `+0.12em` and `+0.16em`. This evokes precision framing labels and museum didactic wall plaques.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the curation of an expansive physical gallery room: breathing room between works is essential to experience each piece in isolation.

- **Grid Structure:** A 12-column layout governed by an asymmetric pacing model. Artworks should rarely be forced into rigid, identical multi-row tiles. Alternate single-column wide salon pieces with offset two-column compositions.
- **Rhythm & Canvas Margins:** 
  - Desktop canvas margins open wide at `4rem` (expanding to `6rem` on ultra-wide viewports) to generate an inherent matte board effect around the digital salon.
  - Mobile drops down to `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters, prioritizing uninterrupted aspect ratios for painting reproduction.
- **Spacing Principle:** Vertical distance is generous. Category separations and transitions between curatorial narrative and acquisition consoles utilize `space-xl` (5rem) or multiples thereof, allowing collectors to pause without visual overstimulation.

## Elevation & Depth

To protect the two-dimensional integrity and tactile authenticity of canvas reproductions, this design system completely eliminates standard drop shadows and synthetic floating blurs.

- **Tonal Stepping:** Depth is communicated strictly via substrate tonal shifts. Overlays, floating acquisition drawers, and filter bars sit on `#F2EFE9` (Travertine) directly atop `#FAF8F5` (Ivory).
- **Hairline Framing:** Micro-fine borders (`1px solid #E6E2DA`) emulate archival passe-partout matting and gallery framing edges, defining boundaries without simulated light sources.
- **Curatorial Overlay:** When modals or lightbox viewers are engaged, the background dims behind an opaque, unblurred veil of `#111110` with 88% opacity, turning the display into a private viewing room illuminated only by the focused work.

## Shapes

The design system employs an unapologetic, razor-sharp architectural geometry (`roundedness: 0`). 

- **Edge Philosophy:** Every container, card, button, image wrapper, and input field possesses precise 90-degree corners. Rounded corners evoke mass-market consumer software and tactile plastic devices, whereas crisp, unrounded edges reference gallery framing, stretched canvases, marble plinths, and bespoke art book bindings.
- **Aspect Ratio Fidelity:** Artwork images maintain their authentic, uncropped aspect ratios. Rather than forcing standardized squares or rectangles, artwork containers adapt their silhouette directly to the master canvas dimensions.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Inquiry / Acquisition:** Solid `#1A1918` fill, `#FAF8F5` typography, 0px border-radius, `48px` height. Hover transitions subtly into `#8C734B` with a 400ms ease-out curve.
- **Secondary / Curatorial Action:** Transparent fill with a `1px solid #1A1918` border. On hover, fills with `#1A1918` while text transitions to `#FAF8F5`.
- **Text Link Triggers:** Set in `label-md` uppercase with a persistent hairline underline positioned 4px below baseline. On hover, the underline shifts to `#A38A5E`.

### Artwork Cards & Exhibition Tiles
- **Presentation:** Rendered borderless with generous internal margin. Artwork thumbnail floats on the canvas without drop shadow, surrounded by `#FAF8F5` or housed on `#F2EFE9`.
- **Caption Block:** Placed consistently beneath the artwork (never overlaid on top). Title in `headline-sm` italicized, artist name in `body-md` regular, followed by a hairline divider and metadata (medium, dimensions, availability) in `label-sm`.
- **Micro-Interactions:** Image hover executes a slow, imperceptible zoom (scale 1.02 across 700ms) with zero sudden shifts.

### Filter & Curatorial Navigation Chips
- **Aesthetic:** Minimalist pill-free tokens. Rendered as flat rectangular blocks bordered with `1px solid #E6E2DA`.
- **States:** Active filters adopt `#1A1918` fill and `#FAF8F5` typography. Inactive filters maintain `#FAF8F5` ground with `#78746F` text, shifting to `#1A1918` text on hover.

### Form Inputs & Consoles
- **Fields:** Bottom-line border only (`1px solid #E6E2DA`) across `#FAF8F5` background, rather than enclosed boxes. Focus transitions the hairline to `#1A1918`.
- **Typography:** Placeholder text set in `body-md` in `#78746F`. Input text renders in `#111110`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Handcrafted square micro-boxes (`14px by 14px`) with `1px solid #78746F` borders. Checked state displays a solid inner square in `#1A1918` with a 3px inset margin.

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **Provenance Accordion:** Documented chain of ownership presented through consecutive, hairline-divided horizontal rows with tabular indexing dates.
- **Viewing Room Toggle:** A full-canvas switch that removes all interface UI, navigation bars, and pricing metadata to allow uninterrupted contemplation of the work against an ivory or deep obsidian ground.
- **Certificate of Authenticity Plaque:** A framed verification card set with an `#A38A5E` double-hairline border containing archival metadata, edition numbers, and curator signature blocks.
