Curatorial Monograph

The Artwork’s Story & Spatial Intent

Written by S. K. Roy Chowdhury, Senior Curator at the Bengal Modern Art Council, for the 2024 Autumn Salon Dossier.

Dr. Gayatri Sen

Director of Conservation

In Silence of the Ochre Dunes, A. V. Mukherjee suspends the frantic tempo of contemporary abstraction to anchor the viewer within the primordial geology of the Great Indian Thar. Rather than rendering a passive scenic depiction, Mukherjee treats the canvas as a physical sediment layer, accumulating organic mineral pastes that have been dried, cracked, and healed over months of deliberate salon work.

Acoustic Stillness

The artist spent 45 days in solitary residency along the sand barriers of Jaisalmer, recording wind deceleration patterns. The sweeping waveforms of the impasto replicate the deceleration of aeolian currents against ancient rock formations.

Luminous 24K Fractures

The inclusion of pure 24-karat gold leafing acts not as ornament, but as an optical daylight collector. When illuminated by ambient sunrise, the crevices catch raw warmth; at nightfall under recessed spotlights, they glow with deep copper resonance.

Every square decimeter reveals over eight distinct passes of rabbit-skin gesso foundation, walnut-oil emulsions, and pulverized volcanic rock sourced directly from extinct basalt chimneys. The result is a tactile presence that transcends two-dimensional viewing, transforming whatever architectural space it occupies into a tranquil sanctuary of reflection.
